    What drives me

    Three things that
    make Eric, Eric.

    01
    🚀

    Entrepreneurial

    Eric has always been drawn to the gap. At 16, he saw Indonesian basketball players without great gear and built a brand to serve them. In New York, he spotted a missing service for sneaker lovers and pitched it into a $1,000 competition win. At BMCC, he saw students with no marketing community and built one from scratch. At Leaf Home, he noticed an attribution blind spot and built GeoLink. At Fox, he saw a retention problem nobody had mapped and drew the full picture. Gap-spotter first. Builder second.

    02
    ⚡

    Proactive

    Eric is genuinely curious and learns things end to end. He does not just do his part of the system — he wants to understand how the whole thing works. That instinct is what surfaces problems early. GeoLink was not assigned. The loyalty initiative framework was not asked for. The AI prototype was not on any roadmap. Each one started the same way: Eric noticed something was missing, got curious about why, and moved. Systems thinking is not a method for him. It is just how he pays attention.

    03
    📈

    Data and Business Savvy

    Eric cares about business growth in a real way. It gives him genuine satisfaction. And he believes that growing a business well means making good decisions consistently — and most good decisions are backed by strong data. Two years running performance marketing across social, programmatic, and direct response at Leaf Home built his instinct for what numbers actually mean, not just what they say. He does not pull reports for the sake of reporting. He asks what decision the data needs to support, then builds backwards from there.

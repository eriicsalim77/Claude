About

From Jakarta to Manhattan

I'm a marketing analytics professional finishing my BBA at Baruch College's Zicklin School of Business — with a track record of turning complex data into growth that actually shows up in the numbers.

I started by founding a basketball e-commerce brand in Indonesia at 16, scaling it to $6,500/month in revenue through influencer marketing and performance optimization. That early hustle wired me to think about growth from first principles.

At Leaf Home, I co-managed $1.7M+ in monthly paid media spend across Meta, TikTok, and Nextdoor. I built GeoLink — a ZIP/DMA-level attribution system that connected media investment to CRM revenue and improved ROAS by 37% year-over-year.

At Fox Corporation, I work on digital engagement strategy for OutKick, partnering with BI, product, and editorial teams to analyze super-user behavior and drive retention initiatives presented to senior leadership.

I'm drawn to roles where data, product thinking, and marketing strategy intersect — where I can own a problem end-to-end and ship something that actually moves the needle.
